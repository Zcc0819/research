2. Risk Management Committee
風險管理委員會
The Risk Management Committee currently comprises Mr. Fan Yan Hok, Philip (chairman), an INED, Mr.
Luan Zusheng, the Executive Director and the CEO, Mr. Kang Guoming, a NED and Ms. Wen Hui, the
Deputy General Manager of Risk Management and Legal Compliance Department of the Company. Its
primary responsibilities include but not limited to provide oversight of the Company’s risk management
programs, and to review the effectiveness of the management’s processes for identifying, assessing,
mitigating and monitoring enterprise-wide risks. The terms of reference of the Risk Management Committee
have been established in writing.

II. Environmental compliance and safety management risk
二、 環境合規與安全管理風險
Description: The regulations on production safety and emission of pollutants were
increasingly stringent, thus imposing higher requirements on the
描述：
refinement of operation of the enterprises. With the increasing number
of operating projects, the basis numbers and difficulties involved with
safety and environmental management kept on rising, and the Company
was under high pressure associated with environmental compliance and
safety management.
有關安全生產與污染物排放強監管日趨嚴格，對企業運營的精細化水
平提出更高要求。隨著公司投運項目的持續增加，管理基數和管理難度
不斷加大，目前公司環境合規與安全管理的壓力處於高位。
Responsive measures: 1. Ideological foundation building: The Company collected safety
accident cases of the Company and the industry, compiled warning
應對措施： manuals, and organised company-wide trainings to reinforce safety
awareness among all personnel (particularly key project responsible
persons).
2. Capacity improvement: The Company conducted hierarchical and
classified safety trainings to consolidate the basic literacy across
all personnel and provided specialised trainings and qualification
examinations for newly appointed key project responsible persons.
3. Supervision closed-loop: The Company intensified unannounced
safety inspections, reinforced the safety responsibilities of the
“top-leader” of each project, and promoted the implementation of
responsibilities through strict supervision.
4. System upgrades: The Company aligned with the three-year
campaign for fundamental solutions and tackling key problems,
advanced refined operation, safety and environmental management
in 2025, compiled and promoted guidance manuals to improve the
level of safety management.
1.
思想築基：收集公司及行業安全事故案例，編制警示手冊，組織全
員學習，強化各級人員（重點項目負責人）安全認知。
2.
能力提升：開展分層分類安全培訓，全員築牢基礎素養，對新任項
目主要負責人實施專項培訓及資質考試。
3.
監督閉環：加大安全生產飛行檢查力度，壓實項目「一把手」安全職
責，以嚴格監督推動責任落實。
4.
體系升級：結合治本攻堅三年行動，二零二五年推進運營與安環精
細化管理，編制指導手冊並推廣，提升安全管理水平。

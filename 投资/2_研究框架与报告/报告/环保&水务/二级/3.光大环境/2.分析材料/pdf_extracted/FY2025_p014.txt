Chairman’s Statement
主席報告
Looking ahead, 2026 will be a year of endeavour, breakthroughs, and harvests for Everbright
Environment. Guided by our goal of building a “World-Class Environmental Enterprise with Chinese
Characteristics”, we will advance the Group’s three major development strategies, namely technology as a
driving force, an internationalisation path, and an industrial ecological system, in a more comprehensive and in-
depth manner. With a focus on winning the six key battles, we will accelerate the expansion of key markets and
the construction of key projects. By doing so, we will strive to deliver better results on the path of high-quality
development.
二零二六年，是光大環境的奮鬥之年、攻堅之年、收穫之年。我們將以建設「中國特色世界一流環境企業」
為目標，縱深推進科技化、國際化、生態型的「兩化一型」戰略，聚焦打贏「六大戰役」，加快重點市場拓展
和重點項目建設，在高質量發展道路上努力取得更好成績。
With our hearts set on a clear direction, no road will seem too long; through steadfast action, we will
surely create a brighter future. In the year ahead, all employees of Everbright Environment will continue to
press forward with courage and resolve, embracing innovation and striving to deliver stronger performance to
drive high-quality development and make an even greater contribution to the building of ecological civilisation!
心有所向，不畏道阻且長；實幹篤行，定能鑄就榮光。新的一年，全體光大環境人將繼續勇毅前行、開拓
創新，以更優業績推動高質量發展，為生態文明建設貢獻更大力量！

Management Discussion and Analysis
管理層討論與分析
During the year under review, the Group’s environmental energy sector contributed EBITDA of
HK$7,109,768,000, representing an increase of 3% as compared with 2024. Environmental energy sector
contributed a net profit attributable to the Group of HK$4,508,260,000, increased by 17% as compared
with 2024. The increase in profit was mainly attributable to the effective reduction of finance costs during the
year.

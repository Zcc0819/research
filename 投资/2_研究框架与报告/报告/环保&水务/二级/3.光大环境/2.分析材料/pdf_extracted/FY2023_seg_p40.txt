Management Discussion and Analysis
管理層討論與分析
During the year under review, environmental energy contributed an EBITDA of HK$9,078,517,000, remained
stable as compared with 2022. Environmental energy contributed a net profit attributable to the Group
of HK$5,271,451,000, an increase of 4% as compared with 2022. The increase in profit was mainly
attributable to the effort in strategic financial arrangements to reduce finance costs during the year under
review.
